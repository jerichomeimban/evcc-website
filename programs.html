Our School

Eastern Visayas Central Colleges is an educational institution in Catarman, Northern Samar dedicated to quality education and community service.

Vision

To become a respected educational institution in Eastern Visayas that develops skilled, disciplined, and service-oriented graduates.

Mission

To provide quality and accessible education through strong academic instruction, values formation, leadership training, and community service.
